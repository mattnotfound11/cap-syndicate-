A crew, not a corporation

Cap Syndicate is a small cap and streetwear shop from Iloilo City. Here's who we are and why we do this.

How it started

It started with a simple problem: good caps were hard to find around here. The ones we wanted were either sold out, overpriced, or only online with shipping that took forever.

So in [ADD YEAR], [ADD FOUNDER NICKNAMES] started sourcing caps ourselves. First for the barkada, then for friends of friends, then for people we'd never met who found us on Facebook and Instagram.

Now we're building the shop the way we always wanted one to be: fair prices, real sizes, new drops every week, and people behind the counter who actually wear the stuff.

Syndicate means a crew that sticks together.

What the name means

Cap

Where it all starts. Caps are the first thing people see and the easiest way to make a plain fit look like you meant it.

Syndicate

A crew that sticks together. Everyone who reps the brand, from the founders to the customer who just bought their first cap, is part of it.

The logo

Brush script: hand-done, like a tag on a wall. It's supposed to look made, not printed.

Red to white fade: from the grind to the clean finish. Every fit starts rough and ends up fresh.

The swoosh and the sparkle: the underline is the signature. The little star on the C is the shine of a brand-new cap.
