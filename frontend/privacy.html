Privacy policy

What we collect, why, how we keep it safe, and how to ask us to delete it.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Cap Syndicate ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information on this website and when you message or order from us, following the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

Who we are

Cap Syndicate is the personal information controller for this website. Business name and registration: [ADD REGISTERED BUSINESS NAME + DTI/SEC NO.]. Address: [ADD ADDRESS HERE], Iloilo City, Philippines.

Data Protection Officer: [ADD NAME], [ADD EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, topic and message.
  • Drop alert sign-up: your email address.
  • Orders and inquiries (sent through Messenger, Instagram or email): your name, the items and sizes you want, and, if we deliver, your phone number and delivery address.
  • Order list on this site: the items you add are saved only in your own browser (localStorage). We don't receive this list until you choose to send it.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may keep basic server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security.

We do not collect payment card numbers on this website, and we never ask for passwords or one-time PINs.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your messages and process your orders, including delivery.
  • To send drop alerts and promos, only if you signed up. You can unsubscribe anytime.
  • To keep records required by law, such as sales records for tax purposes.
  • To keep the website secure.

We process your data based on your consent, to fulfill your order (contract), or to comply with legal obligations, as allowed under Sections 12 and 13 of RA 10173.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We only share what is needed with:

  • Lalamove or the courier you or we book, to deliver your order.
  • The payment provider you choose to pay with (GCash or your bank).
  • Meta (Facebook, Messenger, Instagram), when you contact us on those apps. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Our form or email service, if used: [ADD, e.g. Formspree, Gmail].
  • Google, which serves this site's fonts and map. Your browser connects to Google to load them.
  • Government authorities, only when required by law.

How we store and protect it

  • Messages and order details stay in our business accounts, protected with strong passwords and two-factor authentication.
  • Only the crew members who handle orders can see your details.
  • We keep inquiries for [ADD PERIOD, e.g. 1 year] and order records for as long as tax and business laws require. After that we delete them.
  • If a personal data breach happens that is likely to put you at risk, we will notify you and the NPC as required by NPC rules.

Your rights

Under RA 10173 you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used;
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • object to processing, including marketing messages;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted or blocked;
  • get a copy of your data in a common format (data portability);
  • be compensated for damages from unlawful processing; and
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.

How to ask for access or deletion

Message us on Messenger or email capsyndicate@gmail.com with the subject "Data request". Tell us what you want (a copy, a correction, or deletion). We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We'll respond within [ADD NUMBER, e.g. 15] days.

To stop drop alerts, use the unsubscribe link in any email or just tell us.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to remember your order list. You can clear it anytime by tapping "Clear list" or clearing your browser data.

If we add analytics in the future, we'll ask for your consent first with a cookie notice, and you can say no.

Minors

Many of our customers are students. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before sending us your personal details or placing an order.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Big changes will be announced on our Facebook and Instagram pages.
